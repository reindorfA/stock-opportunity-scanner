How the score works

The 0–100 score is a price-only screening heuristic: 30 points for positive 20-day momentum, 15 points for positive 5-day momentum, 30 points for price above its 50-day average, and 25 points when the 50-day average is above the 100-day average. It requires enough daily price history for the moving averages. This score is not a forecast and does not measure valuation, earnings quality, debt, cash flow, or future events.

For a fuller process, inspect the company's latest filings, earnings, cash flow, debt, competitive position, and risks. Compare companies within the same industry. Never invest solely because a stock scores well.
