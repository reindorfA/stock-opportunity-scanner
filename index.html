How the score works

The 0–100 score is a simple screening heuristic: 30 points for positive 20-day momentum, 25 for price above its 50-day average, 20 for a positive reported revenue-growth figure, 15 for positive reported profit margin, and 10 for a positive P/E below 30. Missing metrics receive no points and are labeled as unavailable. This deliberately simple score does not account for sector differences, debt, cash flow, dilution, valuation quality, or future events.

For a fuller process, inspect the company's latest filings, earnings, cash flow, debt, competitive position, and risks. Compare companies within the same industry. Never invest solely because a stock scores well.
